## 🔬 Featured Projects

### 🌊 [RiverWatch Time Series](https://github.com/alessiamaraca/riverwatch-time-series)

A reproducible R project analyzing historical river discharge data from the USGS.

**Key analyses:**
- Long-term hydrological trends
- Seasonal patterns and variability
- Change-point detection
- Time-series forecasting
- Identification of unusual observations

**Focus:** Hydrology, environmental monitoring, and time-series analysis.

### 🧬 [Freshwater eDNA Biodiversity](https://github.com/alessiamaraca/freshwater-edna-biodiversity)

An R-based exploration of freshwater biodiversity using publicly available environmental DNA (eDNA) data.

**Key analyses:**
- Taxon richness across sampling sites
- Biodiversity comparisons among regions and land-use categories
- Paired comparisons between sampling years
- Statistical testing and ecological data visualization

**Focus:** Freshwater ecology, biodiversity monitoring, and ecological statistics.

### 🦋 RomaNatura eDNA Biodiversity Research

A research project focused on aquatic insect biodiversity and the potential applications of environmental DNA (eDNA) in ecological monitoring.

**Research interests:**
- Aquatic insect biodiversity
- Environmental DNA and molecular monitoring
- Freshwater ecosystem assessment
- Biodiversity conservation

**Focus:** Ecological research, freshwater biodiversity, and eDNA-based monitoring.
